On the second floor of Latine is El Cielo, our private dining room. Hand-painted walls, a single long table and candlelight, with the quiet of the mews outside.

El Cielo is available for lunch or dinner, Monday to Saturday. Menus are tailored to your group, with the full Latine kitchen, cellar and cocktail bar at your disposal. When dinner is done, La Candela is one floor down, with DJs, cocktails and Champagne.
