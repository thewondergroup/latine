About us

The meeting of
two worlds

Latine brings together the elegance of French culinary tradition and the vibrant soul of Latin America in the heart of Mayfair.

The concept

French mastery,
Latin spirit

Latine is built on a simple but powerful idea: the meeting of French culinary mastery with the vibrant flavours and spirit of Latin America.

The kitchen is rooted in French technique, precision, structure and respect for product, while drawing inspiration from the bold ingredients, colours and traditions found across Latin cultures. The result is a menu that feels both refined and expressive.

More than a restaurant, Latine is an experience that evolves through the evening, from elegant dining to a livelier atmosphere as the night goes on, where food, culture and celebration come together.

The story of the house

A Mayfair mews,
brought back to life

Tucked within Lancashire Court, in the historic mews of Mayfair, Latine sits in a part of London long associated with craftsmanship, trade and quiet elegance.

These cobbled courtyards were once the working heart behind the grand houses of the neighbourhood, where stables, workshops and small businesses supported the life of Mayfair's townhouses. Over time they became some of the most characterful corners of the city.

Today Latine brings new life to a place that has always been about activity, exchange and gathering: a historic address turned into a destination for dining, conversation and celebration.
