The story of the house

A Mayfair mews,
brought back to life

Lancashire Court's cobbled courtyards were once the working heart behind Mayfair's grand townhouses: stables, workshops and small trades that kept the neighbourhood running.

Today Latine carries that spirit forward. It's a place for activity, exchange and gathering once more, now with French cuisine, Latin flavour, cocktails and music under one roof.

Hospitality, at the heart of everything.
