The room

An intimate room
with a view of its own

Known as El Cielo, our private dining room sits above the main dining room, a more intimate and secluded setting for private dinners, celebrations and business gatherings.

Hand-painted walls, a single long table, candlelight and the quiet of the mews outside. The room is refined yet relaxed, and lets you enjoy Latine's cuisine in a more personal setting.

Questions

Private dining FAQs

How many guests can El Cielo hold?

El Cielo can accommodate up to 20 guests seated and over 40 guests for standing receptions.

Can the menu be tailored?

Yes. A variety of private dining menu options are available and can be tailored to suit your group, occasion and any dietary requirements.

Is it available for lunch as well as dinner?

Yes, El Cielo is available for both lunch and dinner, Monday to Saturday.

Do you require a deposit?

For private dining and larger bookings we may request card details to secure the reservation, always through a secure payment link or booking platform. Cancellation terms for private dining will be confirmed at the time of booking.

Can we carry on the party afterwards?

Of course. La Candela on the first floor comes alive later in the evening with DJs, cocktails and Champagne. Mention it in your enquiry and we'll help plan the whole night.
