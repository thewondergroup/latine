Questions

Christmas FAQs

How do I book a Christmas party?

Use the enquiry form above, call us on 020 3887 3126 or email contact@latinemayfair.com with your date, numbers and occasion. Our events team will come back with availability and menus.

How many people can you host?

Up to 8 guests can book online. For 9–14 we'll set a large table in the restaurant; El Cielo, our private room, seats up to 20 or holds over 40 for a standing reception.

Do festive bookings need a deposit?

For larger bookings, private dining and special events we may ask for card details through a secure payment link to secure your reservation. Terms are confirmed at the time of booking.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. Let us know about any dietary requirements or allergies when you enquire, and remind our team on arrival.

Can we stay on after dinner?

Yes. La Candela on the first floor turns into a lounge with DJs, cocktails and Champagne as the evening goes on, perfect for carrying on the party.
